一、末影之眼定位（推荐，任何版本都能用）1. Eye of ender triangulation (works on every version)

  1. 准备 2 个以上末影之眼（3 个以上更稳），不需要任何模组。
  2. 站在 A 点，记下 F3 里的 X / Z，抛出末影之眼。
  3. 跟着末影之眼走，当它悬停在你正上方时，记下新的 X / Z。
  4. 把这两组坐标填进「抛眼位置」和「末影之眼正下方」，点「添加两点测量」。
  5. 沿着大致垂直于视线的方向走 300 ~ 1000 格到 B 点，重复一次。
  6. 两条线一出现交点，结果就会显示在右上方。想更准就再测第三、第四次。

如果你的小地图/HUD 能显示朝向角 yaw，也可以只在一个点完成测量：把准星对准末影之眼，读出 yaw（0 = 南/+Z，90 = 西/-X），用「角度测量」录入。

  1. Bring two or more eyes of ender. No mods required.
  2. Stand at point A, note X / Z from F3, throw an eye.
  3. Follow the eye and, when it hovers directly above you, note the new X / Z.
  4. Enter both into "Throw" and "Under the eye" and press the add button.
  5. Walk 300-1000 blocks roughly perpendicular to the line of sight and repeat.
  6. The crossing appears immediately; a third or fourth throw makes it tighter.

If your HUD shows the yaw, aim at the eye and read it (0 = south/+Z, 90 = west/-X) and add a bearing measurement instead.

读结果Reading the result

  • 绿色十字 = 估计位置；虚线圆 = 95% 置信半径。Green cross = estimate, dashed circle = 95% confidence radius.
  • 「所在环」可以判断结果是否合理：要塞只会出现在 8 个环带里（1408-2688、4480-5760 … 22912-24192）。The ring tells you whether the result is plausible: strongholds only exist in the eight bands from 1408-2688 up to 22912-24192.
  • 警告「有视线的反方向才指向交点」几乎总是意味着两次抛眼瞄到了不同的要塞——回去重测。The "points away" warning almost always means the two throws aimed at different strongholds; measure again.
  • 到点后在附近找苔石砖/石砖楼梯，一路向下就是末地传送门房间（12 个框架，需要 12 个末影之眼，部分可能已经填好）。Nearby, look for mossy stone brick stairs going down: they lead to the end portal room with 12 frames (some may already be filled).

提高精度的技巧Getting a tighter result

  • 两次抛眼的位置离得越远越好，视线夹角小会把误差放大几十倍。Throw from far apart: nearly parallel rays magnify every error.
  • 跟得远一点再记「正下方」，基线越长角度越准（工具会自动按基线长度加权）。Walk further before noting "directly below": a longer baseline sharpens the bearing, and the fit weights it accordingly.
  • 三条以上测量可以互相纠错，残差那一列会告诉你哪条不准。Three or more lines cross-check each other; the residual column shows which one is off.
  • 只在下界/末地抛眼没有用——末影之眼只会指向主世界的要塞。Eyes only point at overworld strongholds, so do not measure in the nether or the end.

二、种子推算（1.19.3+ 可以算出全部 128 个）2. Seed prediction (all 128 on 1.19.3+)

Minecraft 从 1.9 起把最多 128 个要塞按 8 个同心环摆放：先算出一个「理想点」（某个区块的中心），再把要塞挪到 112 格内最近的合法生物群系里。本工具复现的就是这个理想点——也正是末影之眼瞄准的位置。

1.19.3 及更新版本：生物群系搜索会从环形随机数生成器里 fork 出自己的随机源（只消耗一个 nextLong），所以 128 个要塞的理想点都能只凭种子算出来，误差 ±112 格。

1.9 – 1.19.2 与 1.8：生物群系搜索直接消耗同一个随机数流，每算一个要塞都会吃掉上千个随机数，所以第二个要塞之后的位置取决于地图地形。本工具只给出第 1 个要塞，不会编造其余坐标。

推荐用法：先算出第 1 个要塞的理想点 → 传送/走过去 → 用末影之眼三角定位精确锁定楼梯口。

Since 1.9 Minecraft places up to 128 strongholds in eight concentric rings: it computes an "ideal point" (a chunk centre) and then nudges the stronghold to the nearest valid biome within 112 blocks. This tool reproduces that ideal point, which is exactly where an eye of ender points.

1.19.3 and newer: the biome search forks its own random source (consuming a single nextLong), so all 128 ideal points follow from the seed alone, within ±112 blocks.

1.9 - 1.19.2 and 1.8: the biome search draws from the same stream - thousands of draws per stronghold - so anything after the first one depends on terrain. Only the first stronghold is reported here; the rest are not invented.

Suggested workflow: predict the first stronghold, travel there, then refine with eye triangulation.

三、常见问题3. FAQ

  • 结果离我很远，正常吗？正常。要塞最近也在 1408 格外，最远 24192 格。The result is far away - normal? Yes: strongholds sit between 1408 and 24192 blocks from the origin.
  • 末影之眼碎了？碎眼只说明概率，不影响方向；多带几个。测量可以在同一次飞行中完成。The eye broke? Breaking is random and does not change the direction; bring spares.
  • 数据会被上传吗？不会。全部计算在浏览器里完成，数据只存在本地 localStorage。Is my data uploaded? No. Everything runs in the browser and is stored in localStorage only.
  • 坐标是不是绝对值？是，主世界方块坐标。下界坐标按 1:8 换算即可（结果面板有按钮）。Are coordinates absolute? Yes, overworld block coordinates. Nether travel is 1:8 (there is a button for it).
